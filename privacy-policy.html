Privacy Policy

Sticky Tasks by Sulaiman Dauda

Sticky Tasks is a local-first Windows desktop app published by Sulaiman Dauda. The app does not collect, sell, store on remote servers, or transmit your personal information.

Local Storage Only

All task data is stored locally on your device in the app's per-user data folder. Unpackaged builds use %APPDATA%\StickyTasks\data.json. Store-packaged builds may use Windows-managed package-isolated local app data. Your task list remains on your device unless you personally copy, export, or back it up yourself.

No Network Activity

The app does not require an internet connection and does not make network requests to any server, cloud service, analytics provider, or third party.

No Data Sharing

No task data, usage data, contact data, or device data is sold, shared, uploaded, or transmitted outside your machine.

Microsoft Store Distribution

If you install Sticky Tasks through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process account, licensing, diagnostics, or transaction data under Microsoft's own terms and privacy practices. Sticky Tasks itself does not add analytics, ads, trackers, or cloud sync on top of that.

Data Deletion

You can remove Sticky Tasks data at any time by deleting the local app data file or uninstalling the app and removing any remaining local data folders on your device.

Developer Contact

If you have privacy or support questions, open an issue at github.com/Sulaiman-Dauda/sticky-tasks/issues. For anything you would rather not post publicly, use private reporting, which only the developer, Sulaiman Dauda, can see.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated if Sticky Tasks adds new features, distribution channels, or data-related functionality in the future. The latest version should be published at the privacy policy URL used in the Microsoft Store listing.
